# Communications Plan

## Audience
- Initiative sponsor
- Pilot users
- Technical leads
- Stakeholders affected by the pilot

## Communication Goals
- Explain the purpose of the initiative
- Clarify what AI can and cannot be used for
- Set expectations for human review and governance
- Share pilot results and next steps

## Channels
- Email updates
- Team meetings
- Pilot briefing sessions
- Status reports

## Cadence
- Kickoff communication at the start of the pilot
- Weekly or biweekly status updates during the pilot
- Final pilot summary at the end of the pilot period

## Key Messages
- AI is being used to assist, not replace, staff
- Human review remains required
- The pilot is limited and controlled
- Feedback is encouraged and will shape the next phase
