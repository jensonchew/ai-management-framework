# Metrics

## Productivity Metrics
- Time saved on repetitive development tasks
- Time saved on debugging and troubleshooting
- Time saved on documentation and test creation

## Quality Metrics
- Review acceptance rate of AI-assisted outputs
- Reduction in rework caused by incomplete drafts
- Documentation consistency improvement

## Adoption Metrics
- Number of pilot users
- Number of AI-assisted tasks completed
- Frequency of approved use case adoption

## Risk and Governance Metrics
- Count of policy or data-handling issues
- Count of outputs requiring major correction
- Number of unresolved review concerns

## Outcome Metrics
- Faster enhancement delivery
- Faster issue resolution
- Improved knowledge transfer and onboarding

## Reporting Approach
- Capture results during the pilot
- Review metrics at the end of the pilot
- Use findings to decide on expansion
