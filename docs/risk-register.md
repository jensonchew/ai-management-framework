# Risk Register

## Risk 1: Incorrect AI Output
Description: AI may produce incomplete, inaccurate, or misleading suggestions.
Impact: Medium to High
Mitigation: Require human review before use.
Owner: Initiative Team

## Risk 2: Sensitive Data Exposure
Description: Users may paste confidential information into prompts or outputs.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Define and enforce approved data-handling rules.
Owner: Initiative Sponsor

## Risk 3: Overreliance on AI
Description: Staff may rely on AI without proper validation.
Impact: Medium
Mitigation: Train users on review expectations and limitations.
Owner: Team Leads

## Risk 4: Inconsistent Adoption
Description: The team may use AI inconsistently, reducing value.
Impact: Medium
Mitigation: Publish clear examples and approved use cases.
Owner: Initiative Coordinator

## Risk 5: Governance Gaps
Description: The project may scale without enough control or documentation.
Impact: High
Mitigation: Use a pilot-first rollout and maintain decision logs.
Owner: Sponsor and Initiative Owner
