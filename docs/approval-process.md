# Approval Process

## Purpose
Define how pilot use, expansion, and exceptions are reviewed and approved.

## Approval Stages
### Stage 1: Pilot Approval
- Confirm project purpose
- Confirm pilot participants
- Confirm scope and controls

### Stage 2: Pilot Review
- Review results, metrics, and risks
- Decide whether to expand, revise, or stop

### Stage 3: Expansion Approval
- Confirm that controls remain effective
- Approve broader use cases or more users

### Stage 4: Exception Handling
- Document the exception
- Assess the risk
- Obtain required signoff before proceeding

## Approval Principles
- No expansion without review
- No exception without documentation
- No production use without accountability

## Records
- Track approvals in the decision log
- Keep supporting notes in the status report and risk register
