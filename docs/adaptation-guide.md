# Adaptation Guide

## Purpose
Help others adapt this scaffold for their own organization, team, department, project, program, or portfolio.

## Recommended Starting Point
Begin with the charter, governance baseline, and pilot plan. These three documents establish accountability, control boundaries, and execution sequence before local customization.

## What to Keep
- The project/program/portfolio structure
- The human-in-the-loop governance model
- The planning, execution, and review rhythm
- The emphasis on practical management value

## What to Change
- Organization name and context
- Specific delivery scope and terminology
- Governance requirements and approvals
- Tooling references where needed
- Metrics and success criteria

## Adaptation Approach
1. Start with the charter and index
2. Rewrite the scope for your own context
3. Update the governance and approval model
4. Tailor the pilot plan
5. Adjust the public content for your own voice and audience

## Good Adaptation Practice
- Keep the structure simple
- Avoid overfitting to one team
- Preserve human accountability
- Document decisions as you customize the scaffold

## Output
The goal is not to copy the wording exactly. The goal is to reuse the structure in a way that fits the new environment.
