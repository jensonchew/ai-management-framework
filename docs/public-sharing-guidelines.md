# Public Sharing Guidelines

## Purpose
Help adapt the material in this repo into content suitable for Substack, LinkedIn, X, or a personal blog.

## Good Practices
- Keep examples practical and clear
- Focus on lessons, not confidential details
- Share patterns, not sensitive internal data
- State assumptions and limits clearly
- Keep the language readable for a broad audience

## Safe Topics
- AI in project, program, and portfolio management
- Human-in-the-loop governance
- Reporting and decision support
- IT leadership lessons
- Practical framework design

## Avoid
- Confidential project specifics
- Internal approvals or sensitive business data
- Unreviewed claims about outcomes
- Vendor-specific marketing language unless relevant

## Reusable Angle
You can present this repo as a generic management scaffold that shows how AI can support planning, coordination, governance, and reporting across different types of work.
