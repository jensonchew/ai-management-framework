# AI in Management

## Purpose
Describe how AI can support project, program, and portfolio management without replacing human accountability.

## Core Idea
AI can assist with planning, summarization, analysis, reporting, and coordination even when the product or delivery outcome itself is not AI-based.

## Practical Uses
- Drafting project and program updates
- Summarizing meeting notes and action items
- Identifying risks and dependency patterns
- Comparing priorities and capacity options
- Improving status reporting and stakeholder communication
- Supporting governance reviews and decision preparation

## What AI Should Not Do
- Make final decisions without human review
- Replace sponsor, PM, PgM, or portfolio accountability
- Approve scope, funding, or delivery changes on its own

## Value in an IT Context
- Better visibility across workstreams
- Faster analysis of large volumes of status data
- More consistent documentation and reporting
- Reduced administrative burden for delivery leaders

## Thought Leadership Angle
The interesting shift in the AI era is not only AI in the product. It is AI in the management layer: how teams plan, govern, prioritize, communicate, and learn.
