# Glossary

## AI-Assisted Productivity
Using AI tools to help draft, analyze, or accelerate work while humans remain accountable for review and decisions.

## Human-in-the-Loop
A working model where AI produces a draft or suggestion and a person validates the result before it is used.

## Pilot
A small, controlled trial used to validate value, controls, and adoption before broader rollout.

## RARA
Feasibility-style scoring used by the portal to assess whether a task is suitable for AI enablement.

## VES
Value and risk scoring used by the portal to assess whether a task is worth pursuing and safe enough to proceed.

## Organizational Team
The function, department, or group responsible for delivery, support, and related operations in a given context.
